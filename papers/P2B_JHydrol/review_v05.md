## Minor Issues

### m1. No discussion of operator training requirements
Transitioning from manual dispatch to HDMPC-assisted operation (WSAL-2) requires operator retraining. A brief note on the training plan, human-machine interface design, or operator acceptance feedback from preliminary trials would help practitioners planning similar transitions.

### m2. Maintenance scheduling not integrated
The HDMPC handles station maintenance reactively (S6: Luodixi offline). Proactive maintenance scheduling—where the Area Agent accounts for planned outages in its optimization—would be a natural extension. A brief mention as future work would be sufficient.
