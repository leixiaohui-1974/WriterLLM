## Summary

This paper presents a thorough CHS-based case study for cascade hydropower control, validated through SiL verification with a well-defined ODD. The manuscript has evolved impressively across iterations: the Monte Carlo robustness analysis (§5.5), DP comparison (Table 7), climate change discussion (§6.8), dual-timescale stability analysis (§3.4), operator training evidence (§6.1), and proactive maintenance scheduling pathway (§6.1, item 5) collectively address all prior concerns from all three reviewers. The structural isomorphism demonstration (Table 5, Figure 5) is a unique contribution that highlights the generality of the CHS framework across dynamical families. The economic analysis (Table 8) is compelling. I recommend acceptance.

## Minor Comments (no revision required)

- The ARI metric (0.82) is a useful conceptual tool; future work could formalize it as a standardized reusability index across CHS applications.
- The climate change discussion (§6.8) provides an excellent pathway for long-term ODD maintenance; the 5-yearly review cycle recommendation is practical.

## Recommendation

**Accept**. The paper makes a clear contribution to the hydropower control literature by demonstrating CHS applicability to Family β systems with rigorous verification methodology.
