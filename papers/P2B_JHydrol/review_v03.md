## Summary

The major revision substantially improved this paper. The DP comparison (Table 7), economic analysis (Table 8), turbine forbidden zones (§2.4), flood routing (§3.3), and hydrological discussion (§6.6) address the core gaps. Self-citation is now at 24%. Two issues remain.

## Major Issues

### M1. No uncertainty quantification in SiL results
All 16 SiL scenarios use deterministic inflow trajectories. The paper mentions ±15% inflow forecast error (§6.6) but does not run Monte Carlo simulations to quantify the distribution of power RMSE and ODD violations under stochastic inflow. A 100-run ensemble for at least one scenario (e.g., S4) would strengthen the robustness claim.

**Required**: Add ensemble SiL results or acknowledge as a limitation with a clear mitigation strategy.

### M2. No discussion of climate change impact on ODD
The ODD is defined using historical operating data. Climate change may alter inflow patterns (more extreme floods and droughts), potentially invalidating the ODD boundaries. Given that Journal of Hydrology readers expect climate awareness, a brief discussion of ODD adaptation to non-stationary hydrology is needed.

**Required**: Add a paragraph on climate change implications for ODD definition.

## Recommendation

**Minor Revision**. The paper is technically sound; adding uncertainty quantification and climate discussion will position it strongly for Journal of Hydrology.
