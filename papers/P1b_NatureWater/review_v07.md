## Minor Issues

### m1. Dual-Principle Section Length
The dual-principle tension justifications (feedback-decoupling, model reduction-emergence, robustness-coordination, hierarchy-autonomy) now occupy a substantial paragraph. While each justification is well-stated, consider whether the feedback-decoupling pair — being the most classical and widely understood — needs as much exposition as the less-standard pairings. A brief compression could improve readability.

### m2. Reference 31 — Brown et al. (2020) Citation Context
Brown et al. (2020) [ref 31] is cited in the "Looking ahead" paragraph for foundation models. However, foundation model capabilities have evolved substantially since 2020. Consider whether a more recent reference (e.g., a 2023-2024 review of foundation models in scientific applications) would better support this forward-looking claim.

### m3. "Companion Paper in Preparation" — Temporal Consistency
The statement about a WRR companion paper being "in preparation" is currently appropriate but will become dated if this Perspective is published before the companion paper. Consider revising to a more durable phrasing: "a full-length companion paper is under development" or including a submission timeline if available.
