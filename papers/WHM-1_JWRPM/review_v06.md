## Summary

This paper presents a compelling three-agent MAS for Miyun Reservoir that successfully extends the CHS framework to large reservoir systems. The functional decomposition (Inflow/Reservoir/Outflow) is well-justified and the 10-year MiL verification provides strong statistical evidence. The climate stress testing (Table 10), multi-reservoir extension (§6.9, Eq. 9), operator HMI with adaptive weight learning (§6.6), and comprehensive data governance address all prior concerns. The paper contributes both to the CHS paper series and to the broader reservoir operations literature. I recommend acceptance.

## Minor Comments (no revision required)

- The functional vs. spatial decomposition discussion (§6.1) is an important conceptual contribution for the MAS community.
- The multi-reservoir coordination formulation (Eq. 9) provides a natural bridge to WHM-2.

## Recommendation

**Accept**. A strong contribution demonstrating CHS applicability to complex reservoir systems with multi-objective optimization.
