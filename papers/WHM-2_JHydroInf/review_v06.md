Summary
The manuscript is now strong in deployment governance and shift-level operability. The added rollback SLO and alarm-state linkage improve practical trustworthiness. I request one final refinement to improve cross-audience consistency and transfer reporting discipline.

Major strengths
1. Governance predicates (Eqs. 4–7) are coherent and auditable.
2. Handover continuity artifacts are practical for real control-room operation.
3. Scope boundary versus P1a/P2A/WHM-1 is clearly stated.

Remaining issues (minor)
1. Cross-site comparisons may still be misread if performance metrics are reported without explicit ODD and authority-state context.
2. The manuscript states transfer prerequisites and non-transfer cases, but does not require structured reporting of failed transfer attempts.
3. WSAL wording in external summaries could still drift toward “level-up narrative” unless tied to downgrade triggers and fallback evidence.

Required changes for next version
1. Add a compact reporting structure that binds benefits to ODD, authority state, safety compliance, and rollback evidence.
2. Add an explicit registry rule for negative-transfer evidence (failed prerequisite cases).
3. Add a communication lock for WSAL statements in cross-disciplinary dissemination.

Recommendation
Accept after minor revision.
