Overall score: 9.8/10
Verdict: Minor Revision

Summary
The manuscript is technically strong and nearly publication-ready. The remaining gap is cross-audience trust governance: external dissemination must be mechanically tied to audited claims and uncertainty disclosure.

Major strengths
1. The governance chain now spans model, shift, submission, and pre-submission audit predicates.
2. Alarm, rollback, and traceability controls are operationally concrete.
3. Transferability boundaries and blocker accounting are already in place.

Remaining issues (minor)
1. Public-facing claims still lack a mandatory traceability token format.
2. No explicit consistency rule checks that external statements are a subset of audited internal claims.
3. External benefit reporting should require uncertainty/fallback co-disclosure by rule.

Required changes for next version
1. Add a public-claim traceability token structure.
2. Add a cross-audience consistency check rule tied to audit-passed claims.
3. Add minimum uncertainty/fallback disclosure fields for any external benefit statement.

Recommendation
Accept after minor revision.
