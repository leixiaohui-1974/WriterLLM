## Summary

This paper presents a comprehensive three-layer HDMPC for multi-timescale control of the Jiaodong inter-basin water transfer system. The manuscript has evolved impressively: the RL positioning (§6.6), demand sensitivity analysis (§6.7, Table 12), formal stability analysis (§3.6), commissioning pathway (§6.8), and cybersecurity measures (§3.5) collectively address all prior concerns. The structural isomorphism demonstration (Table 8, ARI = 0.78) provides a convincing validation of the CHS framework's generality. The economic case (¥13.5M/year, payback < 14 days) is compelling. I recommend acceptance.

## Recommendation

**Accept**. A strong contribution demonstrating CHS applicability to long-distance pipeline systems with multi-timescale control.
